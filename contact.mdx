The Openly Learning support team is here to help families and educators get the most out of the platform. Whether you're running into a booking issue, have a question about a payout, or just need help getting started, reach out using the options below and someone will get back to you as soon as possible.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="General Support" icon="envelope" href="mailto:support@openlylearning.com">
    For questions about your account, session bookings, payments, and general platform use. Families and educators are both welcome to reach out here.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Educator Support" icon="chalkboard-user" href="mailto:educators@openlylearning.com">
    For educator-specific questions about setting your rate, receiving payouts, credential tier progression, and profile setup.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  Openly Learning is currently in early access, and the support team is small but dedicated. Response times may vary — thank you for your patience as the platform grows.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Before reaching out, you may find a faster answer on the [FAQ](/faq) or [Troubleshooting](/troubleshooting) pages. Most common questions and technical hiccups are covered there.
</Tip>

Openly Learning is proudly built in Grand Rapids, Michigan. The team is passionate about making high-quality, flexible education accessible to every family — and they look forward to hearing from you.
