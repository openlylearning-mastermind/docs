Openly Learning is in early access, which means new educators join through a waitlist and are onboarded in invitation waves. This approach lets the platform grow thoughtfully — matching educator supply with family demand so that everyone who joins has a real opportunity to get booked. The application itself is straightforward: submit your interest, wait for your wave, and then set up your profile before going live.

<Note>
  The platform is currently opening to new educators in **invitation waves**. After you join the waitlist, you'll receive an email when your wave is ready. Wave timing depends on subject demand and regional availability — there's no fixed schedule, but you'll be notified as soon as a spot opens for you.
</Note>

## The Application Process

<Steps>
  <Step title="Join the Educator Waitlist">
    Go to [openlylearning.com](https://openlylearning.com) and select **"I want to teach"**. You'll be asked for your name, email address, and the subject(s) you'd like to teach. This information helps the team match you to the right onboarding wave.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive Your Wave Invitation">
    When your wave opens, you'll get an email invitation with a link to complete your educator account setup. Keep an eye on your inbox — invitations expire after a short window, so act on yours promptly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete Your Educator Profile">
    Fill in your full name, the subject(s) you teach, a bio that explains your background and teaching approach, and your hourly rate. This profile is what families see when they find you in search, so take time to make it accurate and specific.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Your Availability">
    Open the availability calendar and add the weekly time slots when you're ready to take sessions. Families book directly from these slots, so only add times you can reliably commit to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go Live and Start Receiving Bookings">
    Once your profile is complete and your availability is set, your listing goes live in the marketplace. Families searching for your subject can find you, view your profile, and book a session instantly.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What Makes a Strong Educator Profile

Your profile is your first impression with every family that finds you in search. Three things consistently separate profiles that get booked from those that don't.

**Be specific about your subject.** "Algebra & Pre-Calculus" performs better than "Math." Families search for exactly what their learner needs, and a precise subject listing means you show up for the right searches.

**Write a clear, honest bio.** Explain who you are, what your teaching background looks like, and what a typical session with you feels like. You don't need formal credentials to teach on Openly Learning, but families want to understand why you're the right fit for their learner.

**Set a realistic rate.** Educator rates on the platform currently range roughly from $28 to $48 per hour. A rate that reflects your experience and the subject's complexity is more likely to earn bookings than one that's out of step with the market. You can always adjust your rate after you've built a review history.
