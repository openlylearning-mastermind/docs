Every session you complete on Openly Learning counts toward a verified teaching record. As your hours accumulate, you move up the credential ladder — earning a tier badge that appears on your public profile and in family search results. The ladder exists to give families a reliable, platform-verified signal of experience, and to reward educators who show up consistently and build a real teaching history on the platform. Your badge is earned through completed sessions, not self-reported credentials.

## The Four Credential Tiers

| Tier | Hours Required | What It Signals |
|---|---|---|
| **Foundations** | 25+ verified hours | New to Openly Learning — active in early sessions and building a track record |
| **Proficiency** | 50+ verified hours | Proven across multiple families — a consistent, reliable educator |
| **Mastery** | 100+ verified hours | Experienced platform educator with a strong, multi-family history |
| **Distinction** | 250+ verified hours | Elite standing — an extensive and sustained teaching record on the platform |

## How Hours Are Counted

<Note>
  Teaching hours are logged and verified **automatically by the platform** when a session is marked complete. The badge you earn reflects real, completed sessions — it is not self-claimed and cannot be manually adjusted. Families searching for educators can trust that every credential tier reflects actual platform activity.
</Note>

You don't need to do anything to log your hours. When a session ends and both parties confirm completion, the session duration is added to your cumulative verified hour count. Your current hour total and your active tier are always visible on your **Educator Dashboard** under **Credentials**.

## Where Your Badge Appears

Once you reach a tier threshold, your credential badge updates automatically and shows up in two places:

- **Your public profile** — displayed prominently alongside your name, subject, and review score so families can see your experience at a glance.
- **Family search results** — your tier badge appears on your listing card, which means families can filter and sort by minimum credential tier when they're looking for an educator.

## Why the Credential Ladder Matters for Bookings

Families searching on Openly Learning can filter results by minimum credential tier. That means educators at Proficiency, Mastery, and Distinction tiers are visible to a subset of families that new educators are filtered out of. Higher tiers also carry a trust signal that can justify a higher hourly rate and reduce the friction a family feels before booking their first session with you.

**Example:** Marisol Ibarra, who teaches Algebra & Pre-Calculus, has logged 132 verified hours on the platform. That places her firmly in the **Mastery** tier. Her Mastery badge is visible on her profile and in every search result where she appears — a clear signal to families that she has a deep and verified teaching history.
