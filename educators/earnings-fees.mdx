Openly Learning is built on a simple principle: the rate you set is the rate families pay. You keep the majority of every session, and a small platform fee is deducted from your payout — never added on top of what the family sees. Understanding exactly how that works helps you set your rate with confidence and plan your income accurately.

## A Transparent Fee Model

When a family books a session with you, they pay your listed hourly rate — nothing more. Openly Learning deducts a platform fee of approximately **15–20%** from your payout after each completed session. The fee covers payment processing, platform infrastructure, trust-and-safety programs, and ongoing support for both educators and families.

Because the fee comes out of your payout rather than being added to the family's total, your listed rate is always the number families compare when choosing an educator. There are no hidden surcharges on their end.

## Fee Breakdown by Rate

The table below shows how the math looks at common hourly rates. The exact percentage may fall anywhere in the 15–20% range depending on your account tier and session type — see the note below.

| What families pay | Platform fee | Your payout |
|---|---|---|
| $28/hr (listed) | ~15–20% | ~$23–$24/hr |
| $35/hr (listed) | ~18% ($6.30) | $28.70/hr |
| $42/hr (listed) | ~18% ($7.56) | $34.44/hr |
| $48/hr (listed) | ~18% ($8.64) | $39.36/hr |

## Payout Timing

You don't have to do anything to trigger a payout. After each session is marked complete, your earnings are sent to your connected payout account automatically within **1–2 business days**. You can track every payout — including the session it corresponds to and the exact fee deducted — in the Payout History section of your Educator Dashboard.

<Note>
  The exact platform fee percentage may vary slightly based on your credential tier and session type. Always check your Educator Dashboard for the precise fee breakdown for each individual session.
</Note>
