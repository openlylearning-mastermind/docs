Your Educator Dashboard is your home base on Openly Learning. It brings together everything you need to run a successful tutoring practice — your earnings at a glance, your upcoming schedule, how families rate your sessions, where you stand on the credential ladder, and the status of your payout account. This guide walks you through each section so you can get the most out of it.

## Earnings Summary

At the top of your dashboard, three headline stats give you an immediate snapshot of how your practice is performing this month:

- **This month's earnings** — your total payout after the platform fee for all completed sessions in the current calendar month.
- **Hours logged** — the total session hours you've completed on the platform to date.
- **Average rating** — your overall star rating across all reviewed sessions, displayed to two decimal places.

For example, an educator like Marisol Ibarra might see **$1,284** earned this month, **132 hours** logged overall, and an average rating of **4.85 ★**.

<Tip>
  Use the dashboard to track which subjects or session types generate the best reviews. If certain topics consistently earn higher ratings, consider highlighting them in your profile or opening more availability for those sessions.
</Tip>

## Upcoming Sessions

The **Upcoming Sessions** list shows every confirmed session on your calendar in chronological order. Each entry displays:

- The **day and time** of the session.
- The **subject and family name**, for example: *"Algebra — with the Reyes family"*.

Tap any session to view its full details, including the learner's profile, any notes from the family, and the session link. You'll also receive reminder notifications before each session, but the dashboard is always the authoritative source for your schedule.

## Review Breakdown

Families leave reviews after each session, and your **Review Breakdown** section shows how you score across all four rating dimensions — not just an overall average. Each dimension is displayed as a score bar so you can instantly see where you excel and where there may be room to grow:

- **Appropriateness** — whether session content and communication were suitable for the learner's age and level.
- **Professionalism** — punctuality, preparedness, and conduct throughout the session.
- **Teaching Skill** — clarity of explanations, pacing, and ability to adapt to the learner's needs.
- **Subject Knowledge** — demonstrated depth and accuracy in the subject being taught.

Use this breakdown to guide your own professional development. Consistent low scores in one area are a signal worth acting on; consistent highs are a reminder of what's working.

## Credential Ladder Progress

Openly Learning recognizes educator growth through a tiered credential ladder. The **Ladder Progress** section of your dashboard shows:

- Your **current tier** and the hours you've logged toward it.
- The **next tier** you're working toward, and exactly how many more hours you need to reach it.

For example, at **132 hours logged** you hold the **Mastery** tier. The next tier is **Distinction**, which requires **250 hours** — meaning you're **118 hours away**. The visual progress bar updates in real time as you complete sessions, making it easy to see how close you are to your next milestone.

<Info>
  Advancing tiers unlocks benefits like increased visibility in search results and access to higher-demand families. See the Credential Ladder page for a full breakdown of each tier and its perks.
</Info>

## Payout Account Card

The **Payout Account** card gives you a quick view of your earnings infrastructure:

- **Connected method** — the bank account, PayPal, or Venmo account currently receiving your payouts.
- **Example payout split** — an at-a-glance illustration of how the platform fee is applied to a typical session at your current rate, so you always know what to expect.
- **Status** — whether your account is active and verified, or if any action is needed.

To change your connected payout method, click **Change** directly on this card. To add a payout account for the first time, click **Connect a payout account**.
