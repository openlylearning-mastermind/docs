On Openly Learning, you control your schedule. You set the weekly time slots when you're available to teach, and families see exactly those slots when they visit your profile — no email threads, no DMs, no scheduling negotiation. When a slot is open and a family is ready to book, they can confirm the session instantly. Keeping your availability current is one of the most direct things you can do to increase how often you get booked.

## How Availability Works

You define availability as recurring weekly time slots. Each slot represents a time window when you're ready to take a 60-minute session. Families browsing your profile see only the slots you've marked open — if a slot is filled by an existing booking, it's automatically removed from your visible availability.

**Example weekly availability:**

| Day | Time |
|---|---|
| Monday | 4:00 PM |
| Wednesday | 3:30 PM |
| Thursday | 5:00 PM |
| Saturday | 10:00 AM |

You can add, remove, or adjust slots at any time from your dashboard. Changes take effect immediately — new slots become bookable right away, and removed slots are no longer visible to families.

## Setting Up Your Availability

1. Go to your **Educator Dashboard**.
2. Select **Availability** from the left navigation.
3. Click **Add Time Slot** and choose the day and start time.
4. Repeat for each slot you want to offer.
5. Save. Your availability is now live on your public profile.

<Tip>
  Keep your availability up to date at all times. Families book based on what they see — if your listed slots no longer reflect your actual schedule, you risk confirmed bookings you can't honor or missed opportunities because your profile looks unavailable. A few minutes of maintenance each week makes a meaningful difference in how consistently you get booked.
</Tip>

## Viewing and Managing Upcoming Sessions

Once families start booking, your confirmed sessions appear in the **Upcoming Sessions** section of your dashboard. Each entry shows:

- **Day and time** of the session
- **Learner family name** so you know who you're teaching

You can use this view to plan your week, review session details, and prepare for each learner ahead of time.

<Note>
  Bookings are confirmed instantly the moment a family completes payment — you will not receive a separate approval request. Plan your schedule with that in mind: when you mark a slot as available, you're committing to teaching during that time if a family books it.
</Note>

## Blocking Time Off

If you need to take a break, travel, or step back from a specific week, you can temporarily remove slots from your availability without deleting them permanently. Use the **Block Time** option in your Availability settings to mark specific dates as unavailable. Blocked dates won't appear to families, and your recurring slots resume automatically once the blocked period ends.
