Teaching on Openly Learning puts you in control. You set the rate families see and pay, every session you complete automatically logs toward a verifiable credential tier, and your reputation is built through structured reviews across four specific dimensions — not just a single star rating. Whether you teach Algebra, Essay Writing, or Beginning Piano, the platform is designed to reward consistency, expertise, and professionalism.

## How It Works for Educators

From application to your first booking, here's the path to teaching on Openly Learning.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Apply and Join the Waitlist">
    Visit [openlylearning.com](https://openlylearning.com) and select **"I want to teach"** to join the educator waitlist. The platform is currently in early access and opens to new educators in invitation waves.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Up Your Profile">
    Once your wave invitation arrives, complete your educator profile — your name, subjects, a clear bio, and any relevant credentials. A strong profile helps families decide to book with confidence.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Your Rate and Availability">
    Choose your hourly rate and set the weekly time slots when you're available to teach. Families see your live availability and book directly without back-and-forth scheduling.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get Booked">
    When a family books a session, you'll receive a confirmation with the session time and learner details. Bookings are confirmed instantly once payment is complete.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Earn Credential Hours">
    Every completed session logs verified teaching hours automatically. Reach 25 hours for Foundations, 50 for Proficiency, 100 for Mastery, and 250 for Distinction — each tier is displayed on your public profile and in search results.
  </Step>
</Steps>
