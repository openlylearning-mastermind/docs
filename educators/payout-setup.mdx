Before you can receive earnings from your sessions, you need to connect a payout account to your Openly Learning profile. The process takes just a few minutes: you choose your preferred method, follow a secure linking flow, and from that point forward all payouts happen automatically — no action required after each session.

## How to Connect Your Payout Account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to your Educator Dashboard">
    Sign in to Openly Learning and navigate to your **Educator Dashboard** from the top navigation menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the Payout Account card">
    Scroll down to the **Payout Account** card. If you haven't connected an account yet, it will show a prompt to get started.
  </Step>
  <Step title='Click "Connect a payout account"'>
    Select the **Connect a payout account** button to open the payout setup flow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your payout method">
    Select one of the three supported payout methods: **Bank account (direct deposit)**, **PayPal**, or **Venmo**. See the section below for details on each option.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow the secure linking flow to verify your account">
    You'll be handed off to a secure payment processor to complete the account verification. Follow the on-screen prompts — the exact steps depend on the method you chose.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm your connection">
    Once your account is verified, you'll be returned to the Educator Dashboard and your Payout Account card will show the connected method and its status. Payouts are now fully automatic.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Connect your payout account before your first session so your earnings are sent automatically the moment the session is marked complete — no delays, no follow-up needed.
</Tip>

## Supported Payout Methods

<CardGroup cols={1}>
  <Card title="Bank Account (Direct Deposit)" icon="building-columns" href="/educators/payout-setup">
    Funds are deposited directly to your checking or savings account via ACH transfer. Payouts arrive **1–2 business days** after each completed session. This is the recommended method for the fastest, most reliable access to your earnings.
  </Card>
  <Card title="PayPal" icon="money-bill-transfer" href="/educators/payout-setup">
    Earnings are sent to your PayPal account automatically after each session. You'll need an active PayPal account in good standing to use this method.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Venmo" icon="mobile-screen-button" href="/educators/payout-setup">
    Earnings are sent to your Venmo account automatically after each session. You'll need an active Venmo account linked to your mobile number or email.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Changing Your Payout Method

You can switch to a different payout method at any time. On the **Payout Account** card in your Educator Dashboard, click **Change** next to your currently connected method. Follow the same secure linking flow to verify your new account. Your updated method will be used for all future payouts.

<Note>
  Account linking is handled entirely by a secure, PCI-compliant payment processor. Openly Learning never stores your full bank account number, PayPal credentials, or Venmo login details.
</Note>
