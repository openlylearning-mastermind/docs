After every completed session, the family has the opportunity to leave a structured review. Rather than a single star rating that collapses everything into one number, Openly Learning asks families to rate you across four specific dimensions — each one measuring a distinct aspect of the teaching experience. You see the full breakdown on your dashboard, so you always know exactly where your strengths are and where you have room to grow.

## The Four Rating Dimensions

Each dimension is rated on a 1–5 scale. Families score you separately on each one after the session ends.

## Where Your Ratings Appear

Your review scores are visible in two places:

- **Your public profile** — each dimension is displayed as a score bar alongside your aggregate rating and total review count. Families considering booking you can see exactly how previous sessions went.
- **Your educator dashboard** — you have access to a full review breakdown, including individual session reviews, dimension-by-dimension scores, and trends over time.

**Example:** Dana Whitfield, who teaches Essay Writing, has 89 reviews accumulated over 210 logged hours. Her dimension-level scores are visible on her profile, giving families a detailed picture of her teaching history before they book.

## How Reviews Affect Your Visibility

<Tip>
  Consistently high scores across all four dimensions — not just one or two — help you rank higher in family search results and earn more bookings. Families also filter by minimum review score, so maintaining strong ratings keeps you visible to the widest possible audience.
</Tip>

Search ranking on Openly Learning takes both your average rating and your total review count into account. An educator with 34 reviews at a strong rating (like Owen Park, who teaches Beginning Piano) will outperform an educator with 2 reviews at the same rating — volume of verified feedback signals reliability.

## Review Integrity

<Note>
  Only families who have completed a paid session with you can leave a review. All ratings are verified against session records — there is no way to submit a review without a confirmed, completed booking. This ensures that every score on your profile reflects a real teaching interaction.
</Note>

## Viewing Your Review Breakdown

To see your full review history and dimension scores:

1. Open your **Educator Dashboard**.
2. Select the **Reviews** tab.
3. Browse individual reviews, filter by date or dimension, and track how your scores have changed over time.
