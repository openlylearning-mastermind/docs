You decide what you charge on Openly Learning. When you set your hourly rate, that number is the exact amount families see in search results and pay at checkout — Openly Learning never adds fees on top of your listed price. Instead, a platform fee of 15–20% is deducted from your payout after a session is completed. This keeps pricing transparent for families and means your earnings are straightforward to calculate before you ever set your first rate.

## How the Fee Breakdown Works

The platform fee covers payment processing, platform infrastructure, and the tools that manage scheduling, session logging, and review collection on your behalf. Here's exactly how a payout is calculated:

| What | Amount | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Your listed rate | $X / hr | What the family sees and pays |
| Platform fee | 15–20% | Deducted from your listed rate |
| Your payout | Listed rate minus platform fee | Deposited after session completion |

**Worked example:** If you list your rate at **$42/hr**, and the platform fee for your account is **18% ($7.56)**, you receive **$34.44** for that session. The family paid $42 — nothing more.

<Note>
  The exact platform fee percentage within the 15–20% range may vary based on your account tier and any promotional agreements active on your account. Your current fee rate is always visible in your **Earnings** settings on the educator dashboard.
</Note>

## Setting and Updating Your Rate

You can set your rate during initial profile setup and update it at any time from your dashboard. Rate changes apply to new bookings only — sessions already confirmed at a previous rate are honored at the rate in place when the family booked.

To update your rate:

1. Go to your **Educator Dashboard**.
2. Select **Profile & Settings**.
3. Find **Hourly Rate** and enter your new amount.
4. Save. Your updated rate goes live immediately.

## Pricing Yourself Competitively

<Tip>
  Educator rates on Openly Learning currently range roughly **$28–$48 per hour**. If you're new to the platform and don't yet have reviews or credential hours, starting toward the middle of that range gives families a lower-risk reason to book their first session with you. As your review score and credential tier grow, you can raise your rate to reflect your track record.
</Tip>

A few things worth considering when setting your rate:

- **Subject complexity and demand.** Subjects with fewer qualified educators — like advanced Pre-Calculus or specialized test prep — can often support a higher rate than more broadly available topics.
- **Your credential tier.** Educators at Mastery or Distinction tier (100+ and 250+ verified hours, respectively) tend to command higher rates because families can see a proven history of completed sessions.
- **Your review score.** A high rating across all four review dimensions signals quality and gives families confidence in a premium rate. Early on, prioritize earning strong reviews over maximizing your hourly price.
