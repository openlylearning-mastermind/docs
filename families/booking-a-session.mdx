Booking a session on Openly Learning is designed to be fast and straightforward. Whether you're scheduling your first lesson or returning for another week of learning, the entire process — from picking a time to confirming payment — takes just a few minutes and puts you in complete control of your learner's schedule.

<Note>
  All sessions are **60 minutes** by default. Session length is shown clearly on the confirmation screen before you complete your booking.
</Note>

## The Booking Flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select a Time Slot">
    From an educator's profile, you'll see their available time slots for the current week displayed in your local time zone. Slots might look like:

    - Monday 4:00 PM
    - Wednesday 3:30 PM
    - Thursday 5:00 PM
    - Saturday 10:00 AM

    Tap or click the slot that works best for your learner. Only slots that are open and not already reserved will be shown. If you don't see a time that fits, check back later — educators update their availability regularly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm Session Details">
    Before any payment is collected, you'll see a summary of everything you're about to book:

    - **Educator** — the name and profile of the teacher you selected
    - **Date and time** — the slot you chose in your local time zone
    - **Session length** — 60 minutes
    - **Total due** — exactly the educator's listed hourly rate (no added fees for families)

    This is also where you can add an optional **note for the educator** (see below). Review everything carefully, then click **Continue to Payment** when you're ready.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete Payment">
    Choose your preferred payment method, enter your details, and confirm the booking. Openly Learning accepts credit/debit cards, ACH bank transfer, PayPal, and Venmo. The amount charged is exactly what was shown on the confirmation screen — no surprises.

    Once payment is processed, your session is confirmed and will appear on your **learner dashboard** immediately. You'll also receive a confirmation notification with the session details.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Adding a Note for Your Educator

During Step 2 (Confirm Details), you'll find an optional **"Notes for educator"** text field. Use this to share anything that will help the session run smoothly, such as:

- Your learner's current level or recent progress
- Specific topics or problems you'd like covered
- Any learning accommodations or preferences the educator should know about
- Questions you want addressed during the session

Notes are delivered to the educator before the session starts. While optional, a brief note can meaningfully improve the quality and focus of the lesson.

## What Happens After You Book

Your confirmed session appears on the learner dashboard right away, where you can see the date, time, and educator name at a glance. You can also manage or review upcoming sessions from there.
