The browse page is your starting point for finding the right educator. Every educator on Openly Learning teaches live, one-on-one or in small groups, so the filters are designed to help you narrow down real availability and fit — not just a list of names. Use the filters described below to match your subject, budget, and experience expectations before you ever click a profile.

## Available Filters

Three filters appear at the top of the browse page. You can apply them individually or together — results update in real time as you adjust each one.

### Subject

Choose the subject area you need help with. Selecting a category shows only educators who have listed that subject as their primary teaching focus.

| Category | What's Covered |
|---|---|
| **All** | Every educator on the platform |
| **Math** | Algebra, geometry, calculus, and general math tutoring |
| **Music** | Instrument instruction, music theory, ear training |
| **Writing** | Essay writing, creative writing, grammar, and composition |
| **Coding** | Programming languages, computer science fundamentals |
| **Language** | World languages including Spanish, French, Mandarin, and more |
| **Art** | Drawing, painting, digital art, and visual arts fundamentals |

### Max Rate

The rate slider lets you set an upper limit on the hourly rate you're willing to pay. Drag the slider anywhere between **$20/hr** and **$50/hr**. Educators whose listed rate exceeds your maximum are hidden from results. All rates shown are the exact amount you pay — there are no hidden platform fees added at checkout.

### Minimum Credential Tier

The tier filter lets you set a floor on how much verified teaching experience an educator must have. Openly Learning awards tiers based on hours logged and verified on the platform — educators cannot self-assign a tier.

| Tier | Minimum Hours |
|---|---|
| **Foundations** | 25+ hours |
| **Proficiency** | 50+ hours |
| **Mastery** | 100+ hours |
| **Distinction** | 250+ hours |

Setting this filter to **Mastery**, for example, hides any educator with fewer than 100 verified teaching hours. Leave it unset to see all educators, including newer Foundations-tier educators who may offer excellent early-access rates.

<Tip>
  If you're starting a longer learning arc — a full semester of algebra or weekly piano lessons — set the minimum tier to **Mastery** or **Distinction** so you're matched with educators who have a deep, verified track record on the platform.
</Tip>

## Reading a Result Card

Each card in the results grid gives you a quick summary without requiring you to open the full profile.

- **Name and subject** — the educator's display name and their primary subject area
- **Tier badge** — a color-coded badge showing their credential tier (sand for Foundations, clay for Proficiency, orange for Mastery, navy for Distinction)
- **Aggregate star rating** — a single score averaged across all four rating dimensions from verified family reviews
- **Bio snippet** — the first few lines of the educator's teaching philosophy or background
- **Hourly rate** — the exact rate you'll pay per session hour
- **Review count** — how many completed sessions have generated a review

Click any card to open the full educator profile, where you can read complete ratings, the full bio, and book a session directly.
