The credential ladder is how Openly Learning turns teaching experience into something you can trust and compare. Unlike star ratings alone — which can be gamed or sparse — the ladder is built on a simple principle: **hours are earned, not claimed**. Every tier an educator holds was awarded by the platform after verifying real sessions completed with real families. No educator can skip a tier, buy a badge, or carry over unverified experience from outside the platform.

## The Four Tiers

Each tier has a minimum verified-hours requirement. Educators advance automatically when they cross the threshold — there's no application or review process on their end.

| Tier | Badge Color | Hours Required | What It Means for You |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Foundations** | Sand | 25+ hours | New to the platform and completing their first cohort of sessions. Great for families comfortable with an early-access relationship and willing to leave detailed feedback. |
| **Proficiency** | Clay | 50+ hours | Has worked with multiple families and built an initial track record. Ratings are more statistically meaningful at this level. |
| **Mastery** | Orange | 100+ hours | An experienced platform educator with a strong, consistent review history. A reliable choice for ongoing or high-stakes learning goals. |
| **Distinction** | Navy | 250+ hours | Elite educator status. Extensive history on the platform, deep family relationships, and a review pool large enough to give you a very accurate picture of what to expect. |

## How Hours Are Verified

Openly Learning logs every session automatically — neither the educator nor the family controls the hour count. Here's how verification works:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Session is scheduled and confirmed">
    When you book a session and the educator confirms, a session record is opened in the platform.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Session is completed">
    Once the live session ends, the platform marks it complete. Time is recorded based on the actual session window, not a self-reported duration.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Family submits a rating">
    After completion, your family is prompted to rate the session across the four dimensions. Completing the rating closes the session record.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hours are added to the educator's total">
    The verified hours from that session are added to the educator's cumulative total. When they cross a tier threshold, their badge updates automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Because the platform — not the educator — controls the session log, there is no way for an educator to inflate their hour count. Every number you see on a profile is a direct reflection of sessions that actually happened on Openly Learning.

## Why the Ladder Beats Star Ratings Alone

Star ratings are useful, but they have well-known limitations: a brand-new educator with two five-star reviews looks identical on a rating scale to a veteran with two hundred. The credential ladder solves this by separating **volume of experience** from **quality of sessions**.

<Note>
  Think of the ladder as the "sample size" indicator for star ratings. A Distinction-tier educator with a 4.7-star average has earned that score across hundreds of verified sessions. A Foundations-tier educator with a 5.0-star average may have completed just a handful. Both scores are real — the tier badge tells you how much evidence sits behind them.
</Note>

The combination of tier + rating gives you two independent signals:

- **Tier** answers: *How much verified experience does this educator have on the platform?*
- **Rating** answers: *How did families feel about those sessions?*

Use both together for the most informed decision.

## Using the Tier Filter When Browsing

The **Minimum Credential Tier** filter on the browse page lets you set a floor so you only see educators who meet your experience threshold.

<Tip>
  For a one-off session or a subject your learner is just exploring, a Foundations or Proficiency educator can be a great fit — and often at a lower hourly rate. For a structured, semester-long commitment in a high-stakes subject, filter to Mastery or Distinction so you're working with an educator who has a deep, verified track record.
</Tip>
