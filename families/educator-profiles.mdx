Every educator on Openly Learning has a dedicated profile page that gives you the full picture before you book a single session. The profile brings together verified credential information, honest family reviews, and transparent pricing in one place. Take a few minutes to read through each section — the details are there to help you make a confident decision, not to overwhelm you.

## Tier Badge and Verified Hours

At the top of every profile you'll see a color-coded **credential tier badge** next to the educator's name. This badge is not self-assigned — Openly Learning awards it automatically based on the number of teaching hours the educator has logged and had verified on the platform.

| Badge Color | Tier | Hours Verified |
|---|---|---|
| 🟤 Sand | Foundations | 25+ hours |
| 🟫 Clay | Proficiency | 50+ hours |
| 🟠 Orange | Mastery | 100+ hours |
| 🔵 Navy | Distinction | 250+ hours |

Alongside the badge you'll see the educator's **total verified teaching hours** — for example, *132 hrs* for a Mastery-tier educator like Marisol Ibarra (Algebra, $42/hr). That number reflects real sessions completed on the platform, not prior experience claimed elsewhere.

## Session Ratings

Below the educator's headline information you'll find their **aggregate star rating** and a breakdown across four individual dimensions. Every rating comes from a family who completed a real, paid session with that educator.

<Note>
  All ratings are submitted by real families after real, completed sessions on Openly Learning. Educators cannot edit, delete, or respond to ratings in a way that alters the scores you see.
</Note>

### The Four Rating Dimensions

Each completed session generates a rating on a five-point scale across four categories. The score bars on the profile display the average for each dimension across all reviews.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Appropriateness">
    Did the educator pitch the material at the right level for your learner? A high score here means the educator adjusted pace and complexity to fit the student, not the other way around.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Professionalism">
    Did the educator show up on time, communicate clearly before and after the session, and conduct themselves in a way that felt respectful and reliable?
  </Step>
  <Step title="Teaching Skill">
    Did the educator explain concepts clearly, use effective examples, and keep the learner engaged throughout the session?
  </Step>
  <Step title="Subject Knowledge">
    Did the educator demonstrate deep, confident command of the subject matter — answering questions accurately and going beyond surface-level explanations?
  </Step>
</Steps>

The **aggregate star rating** shown on result cards and at the top of the profile is a simple average across all four dimensions and all reviews. If an educator has very few reviews, you'll see the review count displayed prominently so you can weigh the score accordingly.

## Bio and Teaching Style

Every educator writes a short bio describing their teaching background, approach, and the kinds of learners they work best with. Read this section carefully — a bio that resonates with your learner's style (structured vs. exploratory, encouraging vs. challenging) is often as useful as the star rating.

Some educators also list specific age ranges, skill levels (beginner, intermediate, advanced), or session formats they prefer. If you have questions about fit that the bio doesn't answer, you can send a message before booking.

## Hourly Rate and Fee Transparency

The rate shown on the profile — for example, **$48/hr** for Dana Whitfield (Essay Writing) — is the exact amount your family pays per session hour. Openly Learning does not add a service fee or booking fee on top of the listed rate. What you see is what you pay.

Rates are set by educators and reflect their experience, subject, and demand. Educators currently on the platform range from **$20/hr** to **$50/hr**.
