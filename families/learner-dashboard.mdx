Your Learner Dashboard is the home base for your learning activity on Openly Learning. From a single screen you can check your upcoming schedule, see how active you've been this month, revisit educators you've worked with before, and track your progress through the learner tier system.

## Dashboard overview

At the top of your dashboard, three stat cards give you a snapshot of your activity at a glance.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Sessions This Month" icon="calendar-check">
    A running count of all sessions you have completed or have scheduled in the current calendar month.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Educators Booked" icon="chalkboard-user">
    The total number of distinct educators you have booked at least one session with on Openly Learning.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Next Session" icon="clock">
    The day, time, and educator name for your next upcoming session — so you always know what's coming up next.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

These numbers update in real time, so they always reflect your current booking state.

## Upcoming sessions

The **Upcoming Sessions** section lists every confirmed booking that hasn't yet taken place, ordered chronologically.

Each session entry shows:

- The **date and start time** of the session
- The **educator's name and avatar**
- The **subject and session format** (one-on-one or small group)
- A **Join** button that becomes active when the session is about to begin

Use this section to double-check your schedule at the start of each week and make sure your learner is ready to go at the right time.

## Saved educators

Below your upcoming sessions you'll find your **Saved Educators** cards — a curated shortlist of educators you've bookmarked for quick access.

Each card displays:

- The educator's **avatar** and **name**
- Their **tier badge** (e.g., Expert or Master), so you can see their standing at a glance
- Their primary **subject area**

Saved educators make rebooking effortless. Instead of searching from scratch, you can go directly from a saved card to that educator's profile and book a new session in just a few clicks. To save an educator, select the bookmark icon on their profile page or on any session confirmation screen.

## Learning tier progress

At the bottom of your dashboard, the **Learning Tier** progress card shows which tier you currently hold and how many hours of study you've logged toward the next tier.

Your tier reflects your time and consistency on the platform — the more sessions you complete, the higher your tier climbs. Educators can see your tier when they review your booking request, which helps build trust before your first session together.
