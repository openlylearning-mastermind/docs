Openly Learning's learner tier system recognizes and celebrates the time you invest in learning. As you complete sessions on the platform, your cumulative hours studied unlock progressively higher tiers — from **Curious** all the way to **Scholar**. Tiers give educators helpful context about your history on the platform before they accept a booking, and they give you a visible milestone to work toward.

## The four learner tiers

Each tier is unlocked by reaching a cumulative hours-studied threshold. Once you reach a tier, you keep it — tiers never go backwards.

| Tier | Hours Studied | What It Signals |
|------|--------------|----------------|
| **Curious** | 10+ hrs | Getting started on Openly Learning |
| **Committed** | 30+ hrs | Regular learner, consistent engagement |
| **Devoted** | 75+ hrs | Dedicated learner, strong platform history |
| **Scholar** | 150+ hrs | Highly engaged, extensive learning record |

Your current tier and progress toward the next one are always visible on your [Learner Dashboard](/families/learner-dashboard).

## How hours are counted

Your hours-studied total is built entirely from **time spent in live sessions on Openly Learning**. Every completed session — whether one-on-one or small group — adds its full duration to your running total. Hours accrue week over week and never expire.

Only sessions that reach their scheduled end (or are marked complete by the educator) count toward your total. Sessions that are cancelled or end significantly early do not contribute.

## What educators see

When an educator reviews your booking request, they can see your current learner tier — the same way you can see an educator's tier on their profile. This mutual transparency helps both sides make confident decisions.

A visible tier lets an educator know you're a regular, engaged learner before your first session together, which can make the whole experience smoother from the start.

<Note>
  Your learner tier reflects **time and consistency on Openly Learning**, not an assessment of your academic skill level or subject knowledge. A Scholar-tier learner has simply logged many hours on the platform — they may be a first-year homeschooler studying a brand-new subject.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Reaching a higher tier can help you build trust with in-demand educators who have limited availability. When an educator sees a Devoted or Scholar badge on your request, they know you show up reliably and take sessions seriously — that track record speaks for itself.
</Tip>
