Your learner dashboard is the central hub for everything happening in your Openly Learning account. From the moment a session is booked, you can track it, prepare for it, and follow up on it — all from one place. Here's how to get the most out of the dashboard and keep your learner's schedule running smoothly.

## Finding Your Upcoming Sessions

After you complete a booking, your confirmed session appears on the learner dashboard immediately. To get there, log in to your account and navigate to **Dashboard** in the main menu.

At the top of the dashboard you'll see a summary of your activity at a glance:

| Dashboard Stat | What It Shows |
|---|---|
| **Sessions this month** | The total number of sessions scheduled in the current calendar month |
| **Educators booked** | The number of unique educators your learner has booked with |
| **Next session** | The date, time, and educator name for your learner's immediately upcoming session |

Scroll down to see your full list of upcoming sessions, each showing the educator's name, the scheduled date and time, and a link to view full session details.

<Note>
  All times on your dashboard are displayed in your local time zone, which is set automatically based on your account settings. If a time looks off, check your time zone in **Account Settings**.
</Note>

## Leaving a Review After a Session

After each completed session, you'll be prompted to share feedback for the educator. Your review helps other families make informed decisions and helps educators grow their practice on the platform.

Reviews are structured around **four dimensions**:

1. **Appropriateness** — Was the content and approach suitable for your learner's age and level?
2. **Professionalism** — Did the educator show up on time, communicate clearly, and conduct the session professionally?
3. **Teaching Skill** — Was the instruction clear, engaging, and effective?
4. **Subject Knowledge** — Did the educator demonstrate strong expertise in the subject matter?

You'll rate each dimension individually, and you can add optional written comments to give more context. Reviews are posted publicly on the educator's profile after submission.

<Tip>
  **Ready to book again?** If a session went well, head back to the educator's profile to book your next slot — you can find any educator you've worked with by visiting their profile directly or searching from the browse page.
</Tip>
