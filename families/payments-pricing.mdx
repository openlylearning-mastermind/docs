Openly Learning is built on transparent, fair pricing. When you book a session, you pay exactly the rate the educator has listed — nothing more. There are no booking fees, service charges, or hidden markups added to your total. Openly Learning's platform fee is a percentage deducted from the educator's payout, keeping your cost predictable and honest.

## How Pricing Works

Every educator on Openly Learning sets their own hourly rate. When you book a 60-minute session, the amount you see on the confirmation screen is the amount you pay — period.

Openly Learning charges educators a platform fee of approximately 15–20% on each session. This fee is taken from the educator's payout and is never passed on to families.

<Note>
  **Pricing example**

  | | Amount |
  |---|---|
  | Educator's listed rate | $42.00 / hr |
  | **Family pays** | **$42.00** |
  | Platform fee (~18%) | $7.56 |
  | Educator receives | $34.44 |

  You always pay the rate shown on the educator's profile. The platform fee is the educator's cost of doing business on Openly Learning — not yours.
</Note>

## Accepted Payment Methods

At checkout (Step 3 of the booking flow), choose the payment method that works best for you.

### Credit or Debit Card

Pay with any major credit or debit card (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover). You'll enter:

- **Card number**
- **Expiry date** (MM/YY)
- **CVC** security code

Your card is charged immediately upon confirmation. This is the fastest option and requires no additional setup.

### ACH Bank Transfer

Pay directly from a U.S. bank account. You'll provide:

- **Routing number**
- **Account number**

At launch, your bank account is verified instantly via a secure bank login flow. ACH transfers may take 1–3 business days to settle, but your session is confirmed right away.

### PayPal

Select PayPal at checkout and you'll be redirected to the PayPal site to log in and approve the payment. Once approved, you'll return to Openly Learning and your booking will be confirmed. No card or bank details are entered on Openly Learning directly.

### Venmo

Select Venmo at checkout and you'll be redirected to the Venmo app or site to complete the payment. Once approved, you'll return to Openly Learning and your booking will be confirmed.

## Security and Privacy

Openly Learning processes all payments through a certified, secure payment infrastructure. **We never store your full card number, full bank account number, or any other sensitive financial credentials** on our servers. Payment data is handled end-to-end by our payment processor in accordance with PCI-DSS standards.

<Tip>
  **Not sure which payment method to choose?** Use a credit card if you want the fastest confirmation and the option to dispute charges through your card issuer. Use ACH bank transfer if you prefer to avoid card fees from your bank. Use PayPal or Venmo if you already have a balance in those apps and want to keep all spending in one place.
</Tip>
