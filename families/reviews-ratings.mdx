Leaving a review after a session is one of the most valuable things you can do on Openly Learning. Your honest ratings help other families find great educators, help dedicated educators grow their bookings, and keep the quality of teaching on the platform high for everyone.

## The post-session review prompt

Once a session ends and is marked complete, Openly Learning will send you a review prompt — either as an in-app notification or via email. The prompt walks you through rating the educator on four dimensions, each on a **1–5 scale**. You can also leave an optional written comment to give more context to your scores.

The review form closes **7 days after the session date**, so try to submit your rating while the experience is still fresh.

## The four rating dimensions

Each dimension is scored from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent). Together, the four scores combine into an aggregate star rating that appears publicly on the educator's profile.

<Tip>
  The most useful reviews are **specific and honest**. Instead of "great session," try noting what worked — for example, "Used visual diagrams that really clicked for my daughter" or "Arrived a few minutes late but was very well-prepared." Specific feedback helps families who read it later and gives good educators concrete reasons to earn more bookings.
</Tip>

## Where your ratings appear

All four dimension scores are publicly visible on the educator's profile as individual score bars alongside their aggregate star rating. Educators can also see a full breakdown of their scores on their own dashboard, so your feedback goes directly toward helping them understand where they excel and where they can improve.

<Note>
  Only families who have **completed a session** with that educator can leave a review. Openly Learning does not allow unverified or anonymous ratings — every score you see on an educator's profile comes from a real session that actually took place.
</Note>
