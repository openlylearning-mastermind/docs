Openly Learning connects homeschooling families with credentialed educators for live, one-on-one and small-group teaching sessions. Whether you're a family looking for the right educator or a teacher ready to grow your practice, you'll find answers to the most common questions below.

## General / Getting Started

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is Openly Learning?">
    Openly Learning is a two-sided marketplace that connects families and homeschool co-ops with credentialed educators for live teaching sessions — online or in person. Families browse educator profiles, book sessions in subjects that matter to their learners, and pay the educator's listed rate directly through the platform.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Who is Openly Learning for?">
    Openly Learning is built for homeschooling families, independent learners, and co-ops who want flexible, high-quality instruction tailored to their needs. On the other side, it's for credentialed educators who want to teach on their own terms and grow a sustainable private practice.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is the platform live yet?">
    Openly Learning is currently in early access. The team is onboarding families and educators in waves to ensure a great experience for everyone. If you haven't been invited yet, you can join the waitlist and you'll be notified when your spot opens up.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I get access?">
    Join the waitlist at openlylearning.com. Once you're approved, you'll receive an invitation email with instructions to complete your profile and book or list your first session. Invitations are sent in waves, so hang tight — your turn is coming.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## For Families

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What subjects are available?">
    Educators on Openly Learning cover a wide range of subjects — from core academics like math, language arts, science, and history to enrichment areas like music, art, foreign languages, and test prep. As more educators join, the subject catalog continues to grow. Use the search filters on the platform to browse what's available right now.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How does booking work?">
    Browse educator profiles, review their credentials, rates, and availability, then send a booking request for the session time that works for your family. Sessions are 60 minutes by default. Once the educator accepts your request, the session is confirmed and payment is processed automatically. You'll receive a confirmation with session details and any relevant meeting link.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How much does it cost?">
    You pay exactly the rate the educator lists on their profile — no markup, no hidden fees. The platform fee (15–20%) is deducted from the educator's payout on the back end, so the price you see is always the price you pay.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What payment methods are accepted?">
    Openly Learning accepts the following payment methods:

    - Credit card or debit card
    - ACH bank transfer
    - PayPal
    - Venmo

    You can manage your payment methods from your account settings at any time.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I cancel or reschedule a session?">
    Yes. You can cancel or request to reschedule a session from your dashboard. Check the cancellation policy on the educator's profile for any applicable windows or fees. If you need to make a last-minute change, reach out to the educator directly through the platform's messaging feature as soon as possible.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What if I'm not happy with a session?">
    After every completed session, you're invited to leave a rating across four dimensions: appropriateness, professionalism, teaching skill, and subject knowledge. If you have a serious concern that a rating alone doesn't address, contact the Openly Learning support team and they'll look into it. Your feedback helps maintain quality across the platform.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## For Educators

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I sign up as an educator?">
    Apply through the Openly Learning waitlist and indicate that you're joining as an educator. Once approved, you'll be guided through profile setup — including your credentials, subjects, availability, and rate. The team verifies your background before your profile goes live to families.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I set my own rate?">
    Yes. You set your own hourly rate, and families pay exactly that amount. You have full control over your pricing and can update it at any time from your educator dashboard.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How does the platform fee work?">
    Openly Learning deducts a platform fee of 15–20% from your payout after each completed session. Families always see and pay your full listed rate — the fee comes out of your side, not theirs. This fee covers platform maintenance, payment processing, and support services.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I get paid?">
    Payouts are automatic and arrive 1–2 business days after each completed session. You can choose from the following payout methods in your account settings:

    - Direct deposit to a bank account
    - PayPal
    - Venmo
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How does the credential ladder work?">
    As you accumulate verified teaching hours on Openly Learning, you automatically advance through the credential ladder:

    | Tier | Verified Hours Required |
    |------|------------------------|
    | Foundations | 25+ hours |
    | Proficiency | 50+ hours |
    | Mastery | 100+ hours |
    | Distinction | 250+ hours |

    Your tier is displayed on your profile and signals your experience level to families. Hours are counted only after sessions are marked complete.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I teach online and in person?">
    Yes. When setting up your profile, you can indicate whether you offer online sessions, in-person sessions, or both. Families can filter by session format when searching for an educator, so make sure your profile reflects your actual availability and preferences.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Credentials & Reviews

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What are the credential tiers?">
    There are four credential tiers on Openly Learning, each unlocked by verified teaching hours completed through the platform:

    - **Foundations** — 25+ verified hours
    - **Proficiency** — 50+ verified hours
    - **Mastery** — 100+ verified hours
    - **Distinction** — 250+ verified hours

    Tiers are updated automatically as your verified hours accumulate.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How are teaching hours verified?">
    Teaching hours are verified through the platform's session completion system. When a session is marked complete by both parties, the hours are logged to the educator's record. Hours from outside the platform are not counted toward credential tiers — all verified hours must be earned through Openly Learning sessions.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What are the 4 review dimensions?">
    After every completed session, families rate the educator across four dimensions:

    1. **Appropriateness** — Was the content and delivery suitable for the learner?
    2. **Professionalism** — Did the educator show up prepared, on time, and respectful?
    3. **Teaching Skill** — How effective was the instruction and communication?
    4. **Subject Knowledge** — Did the educator demonstrate strong command of the subject matter?

    Ratings are averaged across all sessions and displayed on the educator's public profile.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can reviews be removed or disputed?">
    Reviews reflect a family's genuine experience and are not removed simply because an educator disagrees with the rating. However, if you believe a review contains false information or violates the platform's guidelines, you can contact the support team with the session details and your concern. The team will review the situation and take appropriate action if a policy violation is found.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
