Whether you're a homeschooling family looking for your first expert-led session or a credentialed educator ready to build your teaching practice, getting started on Openly Learning takes just a few steps. The platform is currently in early access, so your journey begins with the waitlist — and from there, onboarding is fast and straightforward.

<Note>
  Openly Learning is in **early access** and onboarding users in invitation waves. Join the waitlist at [openlylearning.com](https://openlylearning.com) to secure your place. You'll receive an invitation email as soon as your wave opens.
</Note>

## Your Onboarding Journey

<Steps>
  <Step title="Join the waitlist">
    Head to [openlylearning.com](https://openlylearning.com) and add your name to the waitlist. Tell us whether you're joining as a family or an educator — this helps us match you with the right onboarding wave.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get your invitation wave email">
    Watch your inbox for your invitation email. It includes a unique sign-up link tied to your wave. Invitations are sent in batches, so the sooner you join the waitlist, the sooner you'll get access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Click the link in your invitation email and choose your role: **Family** or **Educator**. Fill in your profile details — your name, location, and (for families) your learner's age and subjects of interest.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Browse or build">
    **Families:** Use the search and filter tools to explore educators by subject, price, availability, and credential tier. Save favorites, read reviews, and compare session formats.

    **Educators:** Complete your educator profile — add your credentials, subjects, hourly rate, and availability. A complete profile gets approved faster and shows up higher in search results.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Book your first session or publish your availability">
    **Families:** Select a session type (one-on-one or small-group), pick a time slot, and check out securely. You'll pay the exact listed price — no surprises. Accepted payment methods: credit/debit card, ACH bank transfer, PayPal, and Venmo.

    **Educators:** Once your profile is approved, publish your availability. Your first booking request could arrive within hours. Confirm it, show up, teach — and start building your verified hour count toward your credential tier.
  </Step>
</Steps>
