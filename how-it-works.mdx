Openly Learning connects homeschooling families and co-ops with credentialed educators for live, one-on-one and small-group sessions — online or in person. Families search by subject, price, and credential tier, book directly with the educator they choose, and leave a rating after every session. Educators set their own rates, build a verified track record, and earn recognition as their teaching hours grow.

## The Three-Step Flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Search">
    Browse educators by subject, availability, price range, and credential tier. Every educator profile shows their verified teaching hours, session ratings, and specializations — so you can find the right fit before you ever commit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Book">
    Select a session type (one-on-one or small-group), pick a time that works for you, and pay securely at checkout. Families always pay exactly the listed price — no hidden fees, ever. Accepted payment methods include credit/debit card, ACH bank transfer, PayPal, and Venmo.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review">
    After your session, rate your experience across four dimensions — appropriateness, professionalism, teaching skill, and subject knowledge. Your honest feedback helps the community and rewards outstanding educators.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The Educator Credential Ladder

Educator credentials are earned, not purchased. Every teaching hour you complete on the platform is verified and counted toward the next tier.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Foundations" icon="seedling" href="/educators/credential-ladder">
    Awarded at **25+ verified teaching hours**. Your starting credential, showing families you have real classroom experience.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Proficiency" icon="star-half-stroke" href="/educators/credential-ladder">
    Awarded at **50+ verified teaching hours**. Signals consistent delivery and positive session ratings.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Mastery" icon="star" href="/educators/credential-ladder">
    Awarded at **100+ verified teaching hours**. Recognized by families searching for proven, high-quality instruction.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Distinction" icon="trophy" href="/educators/credential-ladder">
    Awarded at **250+ verified teaching hours**. The highest credential tier, reserved for educators with an exceptional track record.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Learner Engagement Tiers

As learners complete sessions, they earn engagement tiers that reflect their commitment to learning.

| Tier | Hours Required |
|---|---|
| Curious | 10+ hours |
| Committed | 30+ hours |
| Devoted | 75+ hours |
| Scholar | 150+ hours |
