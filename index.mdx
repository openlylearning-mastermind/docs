Openly Learning is a two-sided marketplace where families find and book verified educators for live teaching sessions — whether that's algebra tutoring, piano lessons, essay coaching, or conversational Spanish. Every educator on the platform earns a verifiable credential tier based on real, logged teaching hours, and every session is rated across four dimensions so you always know who you're booking.

## Get started in four steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Join the waitlist">
    Openly Learning is onboarding families and educators in waves. Sign up at [openlylearning.com](https://openlylearning.com) to reserve your spot.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Browse educators">
    Search by subject, category, price range, and credential tier. Every profile shows verified teaching hours and multi-dimension session ratings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Book a session">
    View an educator's live availability, select a time slot, add any notes, and pay securely — all in one flow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Leave a review">
    After each session, rate your educator on appropriateness, professionalism, teaching skill, and subject knowledge. Your feedback helps every family that follows.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Why Openly Learning?

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Verified Credentials" icon="shield-check">
    Educator tiers — Foundations, Proficiency, Mastery, Distinction — are earned through verified teaching hours logged on the platform, not self-claimed.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Transparent Pricing" icon="tag">
    You pay exactly the educator's listed rate. Openly Learning's 15–20% fee comes out of the educator's payout — never added to your total.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Meaningful Reviews" icon="star">
    Every session is rated on four specific dimensions: appropriateness, professionalism, teaching skill, and subject knowledge.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
