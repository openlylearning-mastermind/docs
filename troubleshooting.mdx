Most issues on Openly Learning can be resolved quickly with a few straightforward steps. This page walks you through the most common problems families and educators run into — along with what to try first before reaching out to the support team.

## For Families

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My payment didn't go through — what should I do?">
    If your payment fails at checkout, work through the following steps:

    1. **Double-check your card details.** Make sure the card number, expiration date, billing zip code, and CVV are entered correctly.
    2. **Confirm your card is authorized for online transactions.** Some banks block online payments by default — call your bank or enable it through your banking app.
    3. **Try a different payment method.** Openly Learning accepts credit/debit cards, ACH bank transfer, PayPal, and Venmo. Switching to an alternative method often resolves the issue immediately.
    4. **Contact support.** If none of the above works, reach out to the support team with your account email and a description of the error message you received.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I can't find an educator in my subject area.">
    If no educators are showing up for the subject you need, try these approaches:

    - **Broaden your search filters.** Remove location, format (online vs. in person), or availability constraints to widen your results.
    - **Search using related terms.** For example, try "mathematics" if "algebra" returns no results.
    - **Check back soon.** Openly Learning is in early access and new educators are joining in waves. The subject catalog expands regularly as more credentialed teachers come on board.

    If you have a specific, urgent need, you can also note it in the waitlist feedback form so the team can prioritize recruiting educators in that area.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My session didn't appear on my dashboard after booking.">
    Confirmed sessions typically appear on your dashboard within a few minutes. If you don't see it:

    1. **Refresh the page.** A hard refresh (Ctrl + Shift + R on Windows, Cmd + Shift + R on Mac) clears cached data and reloads the latest state.
    2. **Wait a few minutes.** There can be a brief delay between booking confirmation and dashboard display.
    3. **Check your confirmation email.** Your booking confirmation email is sent immediately and contains the session details while you wait for the dashboard to update.
    4. **Contact support** if the session still hasn't appeared after 15 minutes. Include your confirmation email and the educator's name so the team can locate the booking quickly.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I want to leave a review but the option isn't showing.">
    The review prompt becomes available only after a session is marked complete. If you don't see the option:

    - **Confirm the session has been marked complete.** Both you and the educator may need to mark it complete before the review prompt appears.
    - **Check your email.** Openly Learning may send a review request link directly to your inbox after the session ends.
    - **Allow a short delay.** The review option can take a few minutes to appear after the session is officially closed.

    If the session was completed some time ago and the review prompt never appeared, contact support so they can trigger it manually.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## For Educators

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My payout hasn't arrived after a completed session.">
    Payouts are processed automatically and typically arrive within 1–2 business days of a session being marked complete. If your payout is delayed:

    1. **Confirm the session was marked complete.** Payouts are not triggered until the session is officially closed. Check your dashboard to verify its status.
    2. **Check your connected payout account.** Log into your bank, PayPal, or Venmo account to confirm there are no issues receiving transfers.
    3. **Account for business days.** Weekends and bank holidays can extend the timeline. If today is Monday, a Friday session may arrive by end of day.
    4. **Contact support** if it has been more than 3 business days and the payout still hasn't arrived. Include the session date, learner name, and your payout method.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My credential tier hasn't updated.">
    Credential tiers advance automatically based on verified teaching hours — but there are a couple of things to keep in mind:

    - **Hours are only counted after sessions are marked complete.** If a session is still in progress or pending completion, those hours won't be reflected yet.
    - **Allow up to 24 hours** for the system to process and update your tier after a session is marked complete.
    - If your tier still hasn't updated after 24 hours and you believe you've crossed a threshold, contact support with a list of your recent completed sessions.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A booking request came in but I'm unavailable.">
    If you receive a booking request for a time you can't accommodate:

    1. **Decline the request promptly** through your dashboard so the family can find another educator. Leaving requests pending is a poor experience for families.
    2. **Update your availability immediately** to prevent future conflicts. Go to your profile settings and block off times you're not available.
    3. **Message the family** through the platform to let them know and, if possible, suggest alternative times that work for you. Families appreciate the direct communication.

    <Warning>
      Repeated unanswered booking requests may affect your standing on the platform. Keep your availability calendar up to date to avoid this.
    </Warning>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My review score seems incorrect.">
    If you believe a review score doesn't accurately reflect the session:

    1. **Check the individual dimension scores** on your dashboard — sometimes one lower score pulls down an average more than expected.
    2. **Contact support** with the specific session date, the learner's name, and a clear description of why you believe the score is inaccurate.

    The support team can investigate whether a technical error occurred during score submission. Note that reviews reflecting genuine family experiences are not removed solely on the basis of disagreement — but errors and guideline violations are taken seriously.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## General

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I forgot my password.">
    Resetting your password is quick:

    1. Go to the Openly Learning login page.
    2. Click **"Forgot password?"** below the sign-in form.
    3. Enter the email address associated with your account.
    4. Check your inbox for a password reset email and follow the link inside.

    If the email doesn't arrive within a few minutes, check your spam or junk folder. If you still don't see it, make sure you're using the email address you originally signed up with, then try again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I'm not receiving emails from Openly Learning.">
    If platform emails — such as booking confirmations, payout notifications, or review prompts — aren't reaching your inbox:

    1. **Check your spam or junk folder.** Email filters sometimes flag unfamiliar senders. Mark Openly Learning emails as "not spam" to ensure future delivery.
    2. **Verify your email address.** Log into your account settings and confirm the email on file is spelled correctly.
    3. **Add the sender to your contacts.** Adding `support@openlylearning.com` to your contacts signals to your email provider that messages from this address are welcome.
    4. **Contact support** if the issue persists after trying the above steps.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Note>
